# Comparison of k=1, k=5 and k=10

`confronto_k.pdf` and `.tex` present the three 90-circuit runs. `dati.json` and `tabelle/` hold full measures; `genera_report.py` and `impaginazione.py` reconstruct analysis and layout. `provenienza.json`, `verifica_finale.json` and `controllo_visivo/` preserve checks.

The stored comparison reports identical scores in 81/90 circuits and increasing token/time costs as k grows. It is an analysis of these observed runs, not proof of equivalence or a new parameter selection. Generate revised analyses in a new output directory to keep the original report traceable.
